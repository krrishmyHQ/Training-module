Supply vs Demand · Two Sides of Every Deal
Supply Side
Developers · Operators · REITs
They build the stage.
Tenants perform on it.
DLF, Embassy, Godrej, Prestige build Grade A towers. Revenue from base rent + 5–15% escalation every 3 years.
→ Developer economics
Flex operators (Awfis, Smartworks, IndiQube) lease floors, invest ₹800–1,500/sqft in fitout, re-license on monthly terms.
→ Flex operator model
REITs (Embassy, Mindspace, Brookfield India) pool commercial assets and pay quarterly distributions to unit-holders on NSE/BSE.
→ How REITs work
Demand Side
Corporates · Startups · GCCs
Every company needs
a place to exist.
IT/ITeS companies account for 40%+ of India's total office demand. Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, and Chennai are the engine rooms.
→ Sector breakdown
1,700+ GCCs — India captives of JPMorgan, Apple, Boeing, Goldman — are the fastest-growing, highest-value office demand segment.
→ The GCC opportunity
Startups want flex — no lock-in, pay-as-you-grow. SMEs want cost efficiency in Grade B or suburban buildings. Both are large, recurring segments.

Transaction Structures · How Money Actually Moves in CRE
5 transaction structures · Click each row for deal context and real examples
From ₹8,000/month to ₹11,800 Cr — all of it is CRE
Leasehold Lease
India's default corporate office structure. 3–9 year terms, monthly rent, lock-in period. CAM charges on top of base rent. Most Grade A office transactions are leasehold.
₹20L – ₹5 Cr/yr
Expand
Flex License
Monthly or quarterly license agreement with a flex operator. No Transfer of Property Act protection — 30-day exit. All-inclusive pricing: internet, power, housekeeping, reception.
₹8K – ₹2L/mo
Expand
Strata Sale
Buying individual floors or units in a commercial building. Like an apartment, for offices. HNIs, family offices, and SMEs choosing to own rather than rent. Noida Expressway saw 18–24% appreciation since 2021.
₹2 – ₹50 Cr
Expand
Fee Simple
Full freehold ownership of land and building in perpetuity. Infosys, TCS, Wipro all own their campuses this way. The asset appreciation on their campuses has, in some cases, exceeded their software revenues over the same period.
₹50 Cr+
Expand
Portfolio Acquisition
PE funds, sovereign wealth funds, and REITs acquire multiple assets in one transaction. Blackstone, GIC, CPPIB deployed $10B+ in Indian CRE between 2014–2024. The institutional playbook that changed Indian CRE pricing forever.
₹500 Cr+
Expand

The 2030 View · Why Right Now Is the Window
India CRE · 2024 to 2030 · Click each milestone to expand
The market you're entering will be 3× larger by decade's end
2024
India's best-ever office year.
72M sq ft
Net absorption. Third consecutive year above 60M. IT/ITeS + GCCs + BFSI drove demand. Flex hit 18% market share.
JLL India Q4 2024

Bengaluru alone absorbed 20M+ sq ft — more than most entire countries. Three flex operators listed or filed IPOs: Awfis (listed, 4.5x oversubscribed), Smartworks (DRHP filed), IndiQube (DRHP filed). Institutional money has definitively validated the thesis. The sector is past "emerging" — it is emerged.

2025
GCC surge. 1,700+ captives target 50M sq ft.
$46B
Annual GCC revenue from India. JPMorgan, Goldman, Boeing, Apple — all expanding aggressively. Every GCC is a potential 50K–500K sq ft requirement.
NASSCOM GCC 2024

India is now the default destination for any Fortune 500 company building offshore technology or finance capability. The calculus: Indian talent at 25–30% of US cost, in buildings that meet global ESG standards. This calculus will not change in your career. GCC demand is structural, not cyclical.

2026
Flex market hits ₹32,000 Cr — triple 2021.
3×
CBRE projection. Operators are building supply ahead of demand — 40+ new centres planned in Delhi NCR, Mumbai, and Bengaluru alone.
CBRE India Flex 2024

The enterprise flex segment — managed offices for 50–500 people — grew 80% between 2021 and 2024. Every corporate that went hybrid now has a "flex-first" policy in their real estate playbook. The operators who built scale during 2020–22 — when everyone thought flex was dying — are now the publicly-listed companies. Timing is everything in real estate.

2030
India surpasses China + Singapore in flex adoption.
Asia #1
JLL forecast. Convergence of hybrid work, GCC expansion, and cost arbitrage makes India the global default for enterprise office demand.
JLL Asia Pacific 2030

The countries that sustained this level of CRE growth for multiple consecutive years: the US in the 1990s, China in the 2000s, India now. This is the third great commercial real estate expansion in modern economic history. You're watching it happen from the inside — and participating in it. The market you're entering today will be 3× larger by the end of this decade.
