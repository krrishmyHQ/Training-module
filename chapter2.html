The myHQ Timeline · Milestone by Milestone

2015 — The Spark
Two IIT Delhi batchmates reconnect in Bengaluru traffic.
Utkarsh Kawatra (Mathematics & Computing, IIT Delhi) and Vinayak Agrawal (Electrical Engineering, IIT Delhi) had met years earlier. Life took them different directions — Utkarsh to Helion Ventures, Vinayak to Goldman Sachs. Then Bengaluru happened: long traffic jams, late-night dinners, and a shared frustration that kept surfacing. Why is it so hard to find a decent place to work that isn't your living room or a 3-year lease?

That frustration was the seed of myHQ.

April 2016 — Founded
A pub becomes a proof of concept. myHQ is born.
A friend offered Utkarsh and Vinayak a pub that sat empty during daytime hours. They turned it into a coworking space, charged professionals a day-pass rate, and watched it fill up. The experiment worked — and became the founding metaphor of myHQ: idle space is wasted potential.

2017–2018 — First Capital
Seed funding. Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru. The platform takes shape.
Early backing from angel investors and a seed round validated the model. myHQ expanded from Delhi to Mumbai and Bengaluru — India's three biggest professional markets. The platform moved from a simple listings site to a bookable marketplace: you could search, view, and pay for workspace in one flow.

2019 — Pre-Series A
$1.5M. Enterprise products launch. 10+ cities.
India Quotient, RB Investments, and LetsVenture led a $1.5M Pre-Series A round. myHQ crossed 10 cities and launched dedicated desks and managed office products — moving meaningfully beyond individual day passes into enterprise territory. The B2B DNA was beginning to form. $1.5M raised 10+ cities
2020–21 — The Test
COVID-19 hits. The world freezes. myHQ bends but doesn't break.
The pandemic was an existential test for every workspace company on the planet. Traditional office demand collapsed overnight. For myHQ, the inverse happened: the platform's model — no lock-ins, no deposits, fully flexible — was built for exactly this uncertainty. Companies rethinking their footprint needed myHQ more, not less.

This period also marked the team's deepest test of resolve. No playbook existed. The founders had to lead through fog.

Resilience
April 2022 — Acquired by ANAROCK
₹125 Cr. India's largest real estate advisory backs myHQ.
ANAROCK — India's largest real estate advisory — acquired a 75% stake in myHQ for ₹125 Cr (cash + stock). The founders stayed. The brand stayed. The mission stayed. What changed: access to 1,800+ real estate professionals, institutional developer relationships, and enterprise sales muscle at national scale.

myHQ became myHQ by ANAROCK — and the B2B chapter accelerated dramatically.

2022–2024 — National Scale
30 cities. Assisted Marketplace launches. GCC demand surges.
National footprint solidified: 30+ cities, 5,000+ workspace partners, 50,000+ seats. The Assisted Marketplace launched as a dedicated high-touch advisory service for enterprise clients — shortlisting, negotiating, and closing long-term workspace deals end-to-end. Global Capability Centres became the primary growth engine. Corporate deal sizes began dwarfing individual memberships. National Scale GCC Boom
Today — 2026
India's largest end-to-end workspace discovery platform. And now, the world.
₹500 Cr+ GMV. 10,000+ B2B clients. 30 cities. 4.5-star app. 500,000+ downloads. Corporate demand now accounts for nearly one-fifth of all commercial leasing in India. GCCs are projected to add 40,000 jobs and 120 new centres by 2026 — and myHQ sits at the centre of that wave.

From a pub in Delhi to the pulse of India's flexible workspace revolution.

myHQ was founded after the co-founders noticed a problem while working in Bengaluru. What was the original proof of concept that validated the myHQ model?
Why this matters
The pub story is the founding metaphor of myHQ: idle space is wasted potential. It was also the insight that flex workspace doesn't require purpose-built offices — it requires smart aggregation and technology. WeWork, by contrast, builds and operates its own spaces. myHQ aggregates and connects.
